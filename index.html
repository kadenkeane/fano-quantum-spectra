Quantum Spectra of Fano Threefolds with $\rho=2$

This page is a library of computational evidence and examples for a conjectured correspondence between the monodromy braid of eigenvalues of quantum cohomology for a Fano threefold and the mutation braid relating semiorthogonal decompositions of its derived category. Data is computed for families in the Mori-Mukai classification using Landau-Ginzburg mirror superpotentials.
